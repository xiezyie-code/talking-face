Talking face

Plug the Arduino Uno into this computer, click Connect, choose the Uno in the list, then press and hold the button on the Uno (or hold the Hold to talk button here), speak English, and let go when you finish. No installation needed. On a phone or tablet you can still talk to the face on this screen: tap and hold Hold to talk.

      When you click Connect the Uno, a list pops up: click the entry with usbmodem or Arduino in its name, then click Connect inside the pop-up. You only need to do this once.
