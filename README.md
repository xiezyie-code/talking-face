# Talking face

A small web page that makes an Arduino Uno "talk": it listens to English through the browser's
speech recognition, answers with the browser's voice, and drives the face on the Uno over USB.

Open the page in **Chrome or Edge on a computer**, plug in the Uno, click *Connect the Uno*, then press and hold
the Uno's button (or the *Hold to talk* button on the page) and speak.
On phones and tablets the Uno cannot be connected (the browsers do not allow it), but the on-screen face still works.

Speech recognition runs in the browser and needs an internet connection. This page stores and collects nothing.
